Our story

A local family. A unique twist.

Arundel House of Pizza was founded in 2022 by a local family bringing its own approach to the house-of-pizza name. Every pizza starts with our own dough recipe, special sauce, and cheese blend.

The menu goes well beyond pizza, with subs, sandwiches, burgers, pasta, sides, salads, wings, and desserts. Whether you dine in, pick up, or order delivery, we are here to serve something the whole table can enjoy.
